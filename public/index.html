What you can ask Tally to do

Each request ends in a real PayPal call, and each call shows up in the chat with PayPal's answer.

“Invoice this PO”

Send invoices from documents

Drop in a purchase order, timesheet or receipt photo. Tally reads it and emails a PayPal invoice with a pay link.

“Refund it”

Refund customers

Full or partial refunds, found from the original sale, including sales the concierge made.

“Pay the team”

Pay contractors

PayPal Payouts to everyone on a timesheet. Batches over your limit wait for your approval.

“Who owes us?”

Answer money questions

Tally syncs with PayPal first, then answers from real data and nudges late payers.

“Only payouts”

Drive the ledger

Ask to filter, sort or highlight, and the AG Grid on your screen changes to match.

“Any disputes?”

Check disputes and tracking

Through PayPal's official Agent Toolkit, running alongside Tally's own tools.

A person holds the purse strings

  • Refunds over $50 and payout batches over $100 are held, not sent.
  • The limit lives in server code, so a confused or tricked AI still can't move the money.
  • You change the limits yourself, and every action is logged with PayPal's response.
