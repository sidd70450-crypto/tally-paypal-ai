What it does

The back office, on autopilot.

Documents → money

Drop in a purchase order, timesheet or receipt. The AI reads it and sends the PayPal invoice or payout batch.

Guardrails you control

Refunds and payouts over your limits wait for one-click approval. The limits are enforced in server code, not just the prompt.
